01 · Creative brief

Director’s music brief

Three original musical directions written to Aleksander Bach’s brief for Tele2’s New Year “Price Freeze 2027” campaign.

Director: Aleksander (Ali) Bach · aleksanderbach.com
“Strong recognisable melody for the campaign. Cinematic, powerful, and unique — people watch the film and recognise the music every time. The music is big; the humor comes from within (their acting).”
Mood references are for emotional scale only — all three demos feature original themes written from scratch for campaign recall.
Stage 01 deliverables 3 demo concepts (video sync + stereo master) → select 1 direction to finalize
01

Cinematic weight

Orchestral scale and serious dramatic weight give the commercial a larger-than-life theatrical frame.

02

Memorable melodic hook

Each demo centers on a distinct melodic motif so audiences recognize the Tele2 campaign within the opening bars.

03

Serious score, deadpan comedy

Scored completely straight so the humor emerges organically through contrast with the actors’ deadpan performances.
